## Visual contract

- **Partial feature study only.** Never the whole shark, full silhouette,
  head-to-tail profile, or almost-complete crop. Artwork must not reveal
  the whole shark through connected or multiple adjacent fragments.
- **Field-notebook look:** loose/confident graphite or ink strokes,
  minimal shading or hatching, no polished engravings or photographic
  texture. Bright plain background, clear figure, breathing room,
  legible on iPhone/iPad. Preserve realistic morphology and feature
  counts even when rendering simply.
- **Text inside images:** only the Latin scientific name, discreetly in
  the bottom-right corner. No common names, captions, headings, or
  leader lines. Explanations stay in the app (EN/FR).
- **Shape:** wider than tall, ~3:1 (at least 2:1; a ~1600×640 working
  export is fine, not mandatory). The image goes **under the text** in
  the research card.
- **Research teaches clues; tagging earns the full shark reveal.**
  Features are educational clues, not always uniquely diagnostic.
  Encounter and Collection keep the complete illustrated animal; Wild
  Archive keeps real wildlife imagery.

## Production workflow

1. Review a small batch of **4–6** cropped studies at the **actual
   research-tab display size**, with real-specimen references.
2. Track `id | feature | crop/view | scientific references | file path
   | visual approval | anatomy verification | EN/FR UI notes`.
3. Check especially close lookalikes: hammerhead head shapes,
   sixgill/sevengill gill sections, shark fin-tip variants, and
   white-shark/mako tooth differences. Galápagos and dusky are deferred;
   Caribbean reef and Pacific sleeper features remain tentative.
4. The six old minimalist SVGs in repo-relative `sketches/` are
   incomplete placeholder starts, not approved finished anatomy
   studies. The approved four above have no committed PNG assets yet;
   their asset paths remain pending.
5. Only integrate illustrations once anatomy and visuals are approved.
   Each image-generation attempt needs Avery's explicit go-ahead.
   Do not merge automatically.
