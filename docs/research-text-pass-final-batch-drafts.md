**Principle throughout:** evidence, not instructions. Every combo element
(region, depth, bait) must stay deducible from the body after the giveaway
is removed. Where a combo element lived ONLY in the answer line, it is baked
into the body (the cookiecutter precedent from Batch 1) and flagged for
Mira. Attractant is chum for all 24; per the audit, scent hints stay only
where they fit naturally and are never forced in. No new biology invented
to rescue a clue — anything uncertain is flagged, not asserted.

**How to read each entry:** current ending → proposed change → combo check.
"Delete" means the entry ends on the sentence before it.
