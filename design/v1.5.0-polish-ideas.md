# v1.5.0 Visual Polish — Idea Collection

This is an open design doc for collecting visual polish ideas as they come.
Nothing is built from here until Avery gives the word.

## Ideas

### 1. Canvas-based porthole waves (for Mira) 🌊
**Status:** Idea — awaiting Mira's take
The expedition porthole should look like the ocean surface from a boat window.
Two CSS-only attempts failed. Proposed: canvas with 3-4 overlapping sine wave
layers, plus occasional splash droplets on the "glass". iPad-performant,
prefers-reduced-motion respected. Avery wants Mira's approach.

### 2. Bubble columns
More background bubbles spawning in little limited columns — a few appearing
one after another rapidly in a vertical burst, then stopping. Like real air
bubble trails.

### 3. Sun rays span fix
Sun rays currently only show on the far left. They should stretch across the
whole screen, fanning out from the top-left sun point.

### 4. Full underwater background vista
Static background from surface to seafloor: bright at top, darkening down,
sediment/seafloor shadows at bottom, maybe kelp silhouettes. Only bubbles
and rays animate. Content should not stretch the background.

### 5. Bigger title logo
The shark logo beside "Tag Along" (desktop) should be ~150% bigger.

### 6. Field guide overlay column fix
Expanded field guide entries must not widen the grid column — overlay floats
without affecting layout.

### 7. IUCN badge color-coding + repositioning
Color-code badges by threat: green (LC) → yellow (NT) → orange (VU) →
red (EN) → purple/black (CR). Move badge right beside the 📌 pin (no
separator) to free space for more of the Latin name.
