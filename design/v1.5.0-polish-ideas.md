### 1. Canvas-based porthole waves (for Mira) 🌊
**Status:** Idea — awaiting Mira's take (posted as PR comment 6093318709)

The expedition porthole should look like the ocean surface from a boat window.
Two CSS-only attempts failed (diagonal stripes, scalloped rows — both read as
abstract patterns, not water). Proposed: canvas with 3-4 overlapping sine wave
layers in different blues at different speeds, plus occasional splash droplets
on the "glass". Must be iPad-performant and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
Avery specifically wants to see Mira's approach.
